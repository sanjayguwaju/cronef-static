New Kathmandu Pre-Departure Center Opens

February 28, 2026 | By CRONEF Nepal Operations

We have officially opened our doors to a brand new pre-departure orientation facility in Kathmandu. This center aims to educate and prepare prospective migrant workers before they sign any contracts or board flights to Croatia.

Bridging the Information Gap

A major factor in worker exploitation is the lack of transparent information provided by domestic recruitment agencies. Many workers are unaware of average Croatian salaries, living costs, or their legal rights under EU frameworks. Our new center will serve as an independent hub where individuals can get unbiased, factual information.

At the center, we will be conducting weekly seminars covering everything from basic Croatian language skills to identifying red flags in employment contracts.

"Knowledge is the first line of defense. By empowering workers with information before they leave Nepal, we prevent countless legal battles and personal hardships down the line."

— Sita Thapa, Nepal Operations Manager

What We Offer

  • Contract Review: Free legal review of recruitment agency contracts.
  • Cultural Orientation: Understanding Croatian workplace norms and societal expectations.
  • Emergency Planning: How to contact CRONEF and local authorities once in Europe.

The center is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, located centrally in the Thamel district. We encourage anyone considering migration to Croatia to visit us for a free consultation.
