National Footprint & Regional Reach

Operations in Croatia

Headquarters & Legal Frontlines

Based in Osijek, our Croatian headquarters serves as the primary hub for our pro bono legal operations and community integration programs.

Our footprint extends across Croatia's major industrial and agricultural zones. We maintain rapid-response legal teams and host community integration events that help Nepali workers adapt to Croatian culture, understand their legal rights, and access critical social services.

Reach in Nepal

Pre-Departure & Community Support

Our reach in Nepal focuses on prevention, education, and safe pathways before workers ever board a flight.

Through partnerships with local community organizers in Kathmandu and regional districts, we conduct pre-departure orientation sessions. We educate prospective migrants on Croatian labor laws, identify predatory recruitment agencies, and ensure workers are fully prepared for their international journey.

A registered NGO in Osijek, Croatia, supporting safe migration and pro bono legal aid for Nepal's workforce.
