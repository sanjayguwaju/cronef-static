Who We Are

Founded on the belief that safe migration is a fundamental right, CRONEF bridges the geographical and legal divide between Nepal and Croatia.

We are a dedicated team of legal experts, community organizers, and translators who understand both sides of the journey. By working directly with workers, employers, and authorities, we eliminate the exploitation that often occurs during international recruitment.

Bridging Borders • Building Trust

Our Mission

To provide pro bono legal aid and ensure fair treatment for Nepali workers in Croatia.

Our Vision

A future where every migrant worker can thrive in a safe and legally protected environment.

A registered NGO in Osijek, Croatia, supporting safe migration and pro bono legal aid for Nepal's workforce.
