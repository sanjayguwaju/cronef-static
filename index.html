Our Mandate

Three commitments, written into how we work.

Everything CRONEF does traces back to one of three articles — the same order we use when we open a new case file.

Article I

Safe migration pathways

We help workers verify recruiters, contracts, and job offers before departure, so the journey to Croatia starts on fair, legal terms — not a debt trap.

Article II

Pro bono legal aid

Free, confidential representation for wage theft, unsafe conditions, contract breaches, and residency issues — in a language workers actually speak.

Article III

Community integration

Language support, housing guidance, and a network of Nepali and Croatian neighbours so newcomers aren't navigating a new country alone.

How Legal Aid Works

From a first message to a resolved case.

A straightforward intake process, built so a worker with limited time or documentation can still get help.

01

Reach out

Call, message, or visit our Osijek office. No paperwork required to start — just tell us what's happening.

02

Confidential review

A caseworker and legal volunteer review your contract, documents, and situation within 48 hours.

03

Free representation

We advocate directly with employers, agencies, or authorities on your behalf, at no cost to you.

04

Resolution & follow-up

We stay engaged until the issue is resolved, and check back in to make sure it stays that way.

About CRONEF

Built for the distance between two countries.

As more Nepali workers come to Croatia for jobs in construction, hospitality, and industry, the gap between a signed contract and the reality on the ground can be wide — and dangerous to navigate alone. CRONEF exists to close that gap: with legal knowledge, language, and people who understand both sides of the journey.

We work with workers directly, and alongside employers, agencies, and Croatian authorities who share our interest in migration that is safe, legal, and fair.
